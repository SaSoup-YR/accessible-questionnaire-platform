## Evidence rule

AI output, source code, and automated tests are not treated as evidence that a real assistive technology worked. Manual claims use the recorded observation from the named device, browser, and assistive-technology route. Unsupported repair attempts remain documented as failures or are excluded from the executable release.

AI assistance must not be used to invent participant data, manual observations, source content, quotations, references, or verification that did not occur.
