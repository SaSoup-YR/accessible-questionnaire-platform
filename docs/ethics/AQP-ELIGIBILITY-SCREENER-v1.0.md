# Eligibility Screener

**Study:** Accessible Questionnaire Platform — observed researcher study  
**Version:** 1.0, 10 August 2026  
**Complete only through the approved recruitment route.**

Use Yes or No unless another answer is requested. These questions decide whether
the study matches your experience. They do not test your ability.

1. Are you 18 or over?  Yes / No
2. Can you independently use a desktop or laptop web browser?  Yes / No
3. Have you helped to design or develop AQP?  Yes / No
4. How many online questionnaire studies have you previously set up?
   0 / 1 / 2 / 3 or more
5. Can you use the approved session language well enough to follow the task sheet?
   Yes / No
6. Have you read that the study window and session audio must be recorded for
   coding, and are you willing to consider that after reading the full information
   sheet?  Yes / No
7. Which questionnaire tools have you used, if any?  Short answer (optional)
8. Which desktop/laptop browser would you normally use?  Short answer

## Researcher-only eligibility rule

Eligible only when Q1, Q2, Q5 and Q6 are Yes; Q3 is No; and Q4 is 0, 1 or 2.
Do not collect a diagnosis or infer disability. Do not retain ineligible responses
longer than the approved recruitment record requires. Send the information sheet
and consent form before arranging a session; screening is not consent.
