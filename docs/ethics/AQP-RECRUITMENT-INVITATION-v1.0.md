## Help us test two questionnaire-setup tools

We are looking for 6–8 adults who are new to setting up online questionnaire
studies. The study tests the software, not you.

You may be eligible if you:

- are 18 or over;
- can use a desktop or laptop browser;
- have set up no more than two online questionnaire studies; and
- did not help to develop AQP.

The remote session takes up to 75 minutes. You will set up the same short
questionnaire in AQP and a prepared UCL Qualtrics template, check one imported
setup, give brief ratings and answer three short questions.

The study window and session audio must be recorded so task time, assistance and
errors can be checked. You do not have to agree. If you do not agree to recording,
you will not take part, and there is no penalty. No camera recording is planned.

Taking part is voluntary. You may pause or stop at any time. No payment is planned.

To receive the information sheet and eligibility questions, contact Dr Mark Colley
at m.colley@ucl.ac.uk. Please do not send a diagnosis, health information or any
research-participant data.
