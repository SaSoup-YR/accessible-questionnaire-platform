Scoring for the researcher: odd-item contribution = response minus 1; even-item
contribution = 5 minus response; sum all ten contributions and multiply by 2.5.
If any item is `M`, record the scale score as `M`; do not impute.

## Source and fixed-use boundary

The SUS wording and scoring follow [Brooke, J. (1996), *SUS: A quick and dirty
usability scale*](https://digital.ahrq.gov/sites/default/files/docs/survey/systemusabilityscale%2528sus%2529_comp%255B1%255D.pdf).
The SEQ is a frozen seven-point post-task item informed by [Sauro, J. and Dumas,
J. S. (2009), “Comparison of three one-question, post-task usability
questionnaires”](https://doi.org/10.1145/1518701.1518946).

This sheet records immediate self-report. It does not determine observed task
success and does not replace time, assist, error or end-state verification data.
