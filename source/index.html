Research prototype

Accessible Questionnaire Platform

AQP runs compatible questionnaire definitions through one participant interface. Researchers can use a built-in definition, review a supported source export, or build a small questionnaire within the documented profile.

Public entry-point release: v0.8.1

Questionnaire demonstrations

Each demonstration uses browser-local storage only. It does not upload answers or act as a research-data system.

Open researcher setup

Participant links

Participants normally open a link generated by the researcher setup page. When a valid study configuration is present in the link, this page opens the shared participant runner directly.

The released evidence is limited to the documented configurations and does not establish universal accessibility, complete WCAG conformance, psychometric equivalence, or benefit for disabled users.
